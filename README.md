An offline guide and tracker for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI trophies on PlayStation 5 — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden, 27 visible; all single-player, none online.

Every trophy has its own guide, written in our own words and citing its source (Exophase, HowToVideoGame, OutputLag, EGamers, u7buy). The app includes a checklist with progress saved in your browser, export/import of progress as JSON, filters by grade, category, hidden/visible, missable and done/not done, text search, a spoiler mode that hides un-earned hidden and story trophies, and an 8-step roadmap to the platinum with the one possibly-missable trophy — Positive Mental Attitude — flagged prominently, with manual-save advice. The five trophies whose unlock method was unconfirmed at launch say so plainly instead of guessing.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open <http://localhost:8080>. Or open `app/index.html` directly in a browser — everything loads from local files, no network needed.

## Data

Trophy names, descriptions and grades follow the official 55-trophy list as published on Exophase (18 Nov 2026). The data lives in `app/data/trophies.json`. Estimated time to platinum: about 200 hours (HowToVideoGame).
