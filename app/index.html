Roadmap to the platinum

Play in this order. The story trophies cannot be missed, but one side trophy can — read the warning in step 1.

    ⚠ Possibly missable — Positive Mental Attitude. Meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. Lucia's balcony meditation spot in Chapter 1 counts toward it and disappears after that chapter, and there is no mission replay. Meditate there before Chapter 1 ends, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter plus one before the final mission. Reported by OutputLag and EGamers; spot count from HowToVideoGame.
